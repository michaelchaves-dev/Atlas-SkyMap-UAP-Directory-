How verification works

  1. Timestamp first. An entry with no reliable date, time and place does not enter the register.
  2. Satellite correlation. The time and position are checked against published satellite tracks — most “trains of lights” resolve here.
  3. Aircraft & balloons. Flight activity and known balloon transits are checked next.
  4. Space weather. Solar and geomagnetic conditions from NOAA are noted for light-phenomenon reports.
  5. Honest label. Explained, Mixed evidence, Government released, or Unresolved — and the correlation note says exactly what was checked.
